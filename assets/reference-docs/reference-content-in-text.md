2. The highest respect for the process of pregnancy, birth, and becoming a mother.

### Image: Our Path and Training
**OUR JOURNEY AND TRAINING**

We are a group of three midwives, trained in Spain and the United Kingdom, with extensive experience in pregnancy and postpartum follow-up, as well as hospital and home birth care. We currently combine our clinical work with workshops on preparing for motherhood and fatherhood, and on breastfeeding/formula feeding, collaborating with other professionals dedicated to women’s care. We also provide private home visits to answer questions, advise on and assess breastfeeding, external cephalic version, or any issue that may arise during pregnancy, postpartum, and parenting.

- Theoretical and practical training in home birth assistance through ALPACC.  
- Experience and training in water birth assistance.  
- Advice on tongue-tie, breastfeeding, weaning, and newborn feeding.  
- Training in rebozo and fetal and maternal biomechanics.  
- Guidance on baby-led weaning and complementary feeding.  
- Training in physical conditioning during pregnancy using the AIPAP Agua y Terra method.  
- Experience in complementary techniques and therapies: hypnobirthing, acupuncture, and acupressure.

### Profile: Isabel
**MEET ISABEL**

My name is Isabel. I am a midwife and nurse, an educator, and I am passionate about my work.

My main goal is for women to have access to truthful and objective information based on scientific evidence, so that they can be free to make decisions about their motherhood, with themselves and their children at the centre.

Pregnancy, birth, and postpartum are intense stages that fill us with emotion, but also with doubts and even fears.

I want you to know that the person who knows most about your pregnancy, your birth, and your baby is you. The midwife or gynaecologist is there to accompany you through the process and give you information and support when you need it.  
But when the moment comes, even if it is your first time, you will know how to do it.

Pregnancy and birth are instinctive processes. Trust the wisdom of your body.

**Associated quote:**  
“The best environment for giving birth is exactly the same environment in which one makes love.”

My commitment is to preserve the innate wisdom of your body, to be by your side so that you feel safe, heard, and supported, intervening only when strictly necessary and always through consensus and respect. Remember that the power has always been yours.
