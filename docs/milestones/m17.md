# M17 — Information Architecture Audit

## Goal

Decide whether the site should remain a single-page experience or evolve into a small multi-page site as content grows.

## Recommended Delivery Mode

- Lead: Codex Sonnet or stronger, with human approval
- Inputs: M13 benchmark findings, M14 personas, and current content roadmap

## Dependencies

- M13 complete
- M14 complete
- M15 and M16 recommended

## PR Scope

- `docs/research/m17-ia-audit.md`
- `docs/PRD.md` — update section 10 if the recommended structure changes
- `docs/plan.md` — optional dependency notes for later milestones

## Tasks

- [ ] Audit the current single-page experience against the known and planned content surface
- [ ] Evaluate whether services, education, resources, and legal pages still fit the current IA comfortably
- [ ] Compare at least three IA options:
  keep single-page, hybrid single-page plus detail pages, or fuller multi-page split
- [ ] Score each option against:
  clarity, ease of maintenance, mobile flow, SEO implications, and founder editing burden
- [ ] End with one recommended direction and a short rationale
- [ ] Do not redesign the site in this milestone; this is a decision document only

## Definition of Done

- A reviewer can make a confident IA decision from one document
- The recommendation is grounded in the actual roadmap, not generic web advice
- Later content milestones know whether to target a section, a detail page, or a hybrid structure

## Open Questions

- If we choose a hybrid structure, which content deserves its own page first: services, resources, or education?
