# M17 — Information Architecture Audit

## Goal

Decide whether the site should remain a single-page experience or evolve into a small multi-page site as content grows.

## Recommended Delivery Mode

- Lead: Codex Sonnet or stronger, with human approval
- Inputs: M13 benchmark findings, M14 personas, and current content roadmap

## Dependencies

- M13 complete ✓
- M14 complete ✓
- M17A complete ✓ (FAQ surface)
- M17B complete ✓ (How We Work / Safety)
- M18 complete ✓ (Education and credentials)
- M19 complete ✓ (Resources library)
- M32 complete ✓ (Mobile UX pass)
- M15 (Services rewrite) — not yet done; services content is still v0 placeholder quality
- M16 (WhatsApp feasibility) — not yet done; contact channels still email + Instagram only

## PR Scope

- `docs/research/m17-ia-audit.md`
- `docs/PRD.md` — update section 10 if the recommended structure changes
- `docs/plan.md` — optional dependency notes for later milestones

## Tasks

- [ ] Audit the full current content surface: hero, philosophy, why-alumbra, team, credentials, services, how-we-work, FAQ, events, resources, brochure, testimonials, contact, footer
- [ ] Assess current single-page scroll depth and cognitive load — is the page too long?
- [ ] Evaluate whether services, education, resources, FAQ, and legal pages still fit single-page comfortably as content matures
- [ ] Compare at least three IA options: keep single-page, hybrid (single-page + select detail pages), or fuller multi-page split
- [ ] Score each option against: clarity for 13 personas, mobile flow, SEO implications, maintainability, founder editing burden, i18n complexity (ES → EN → CA)
- [ ] Assess navigation: does current anchor nav still work at full content scale? Would a mega-nav, tabbed sections, or a separate nav per page work better?
- [ ] Consider internal linking needs: services ↔ FAQ ↔ how-we-work ↔ contact; would cross-links improve journeys?
- [ ] Consider sitemap needs: does a multi-page structure require a sitemap.xml for SEO?
- [ ] Anticipate future content growth: more resources/blog posts, pricing tiers, downloadables, English and Valencian content, more events — will the current structure carry this?
- [ ] End with one recommended direction, a clear rationale, and a proposed page/section map if multi-page is recommended
- [ ] Do not produce implementation specs; this is a decision and planning document only

## Definition of Done

- A reviewer can make a confident IA decision from one document
- The recommendation is grounded in the actual roadmap and 13 personas, not generic web advice
- The document covers: scroll depth assessment, nav scalability, internal linking needs, sitemap needs, i18n implications, and future content growth
- Later milestones (M20 SEO, M25/M26 languages, M27 accessibility) know whether to target a section, a detail page, or a hybrid structure

## Open Questions

- If we choose a hybrid structure, which content deserves its own page first: services, resources, or education?
- Should the FAQ be a standalone page (better for SEO, direct linking) or stay as a homepage section?
- Should legal pages (privacy, cookies) be factored into the navigation or remain footer-only links?
- How many blog/resource posts are anticipated in the first year — does that volume justify a listing page?
