# M17.5 — `/formacion` Standalone Page (Optional)

## Goal

Split the credentials and education surface to its own page (`/formacion.html`) once real credential data is populated by the founders. The homepage keeps the formación section as credential-badge summaries on the team cards.

**This milestone should not be spawned until real credentials are confirmed.** Until then the formación section stays on the homepage as expanded team cards (current state). Spawning this milestone with all-placeholder credentials produces a thin page that undermines trust rather than building it.

## Dependencies

- M17.4 complete
- Real credential data confirmed in `content/team.json` for all three midwives:
  - `registration` field populated (colegiada number or professional registration)
  - At minimum one `credentials` item that is not `placeholder: true`
  - At minimum one `training` item that is not `placeholder: true`

## PR Scope

- New `formacion.html` — top-level page scaffold. Loads per-midwife credential data from `content/team.json`.
- `js/content-loader.js` — extended for per-page formacion context: full credential display on `/formacion.html`, badge summary on homepage
- `<title>`, meta description, og tags for `formacion.html`
- Homepage `sections/formacion.html` — becomes credential-badge summary (registration status, language indicator, 1–2 credential badges per midwife)
- Footer nav gains Formación link
- Internal cross-links: `formacion.html` → `/servicios.html` (specialisms link to services); team section on homepage links to `formacion.html` with "ver formación y credenciales →"

- `/formacion.html` renders real (non-placeholder) credentials for all three midwives
- Each midwife has at minimum: one registration number and one named credential
- No `__PLACEHOLDER__` values rendered on the page (credential fields that are placeholder are hidden, not labelled)
