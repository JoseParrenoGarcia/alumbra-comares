# M24 — Privacy Policy Audit and Rewrite

## Goal

Rewrite `privacidad.html` so the privacy language is proportionate to the actual site and contact workflow: protective enough to avoid careless promises, but not written like a large corporate platform.

## Recommended Delivery Mode

- Lead: human-led policy review plus Codex Sonnet rewrite
- Not Haiku-led: this milestone needs careful language tradeoffs and current-facts validation

## Dependencies

- M12 complete
- M16 complete if chat channels are added
- M23 recommended first

## PR Scope

- `privacidad.html`
- `content/contact.json` only if contact process wording needs to align
- Optional short audit note in `docs/research/m24-privacy-audit.md`

## Tasks

- [ ] Inventory what personal data the site actually collects or routes:
  contact form fields, destination inbox, phone/WhatsApp handling, and any third-party processors
- [ ] Rewrite the privacy page in plain Spanish around the real workflow
- [ ] Avoid promises the team cannot realistically operationalize
- [ ] Avoid false minimization if personal data is in fact being collected
- [ ] Keep the draft lightweight, readable, and clearly bounded to the site’s current behavior
- [ ] Flag anything that may need later professional legal review

## Human Review Prompt

```text
Help me draft a proportionate privacy policy for a very small Spanish website for an independent midwife collective.

The site is simple and may include:
- a contact form via Formspree or similar
- email and phone contact details
- possibly WhatsApp or Telegram links
- no accounts, no patient portal, no ecommerce

I want:
1. A practical list of what must be covered
2. Language that is clear and modest, not ultra-corporate
3. Warnings about overpromising GDPR operations we may not have
4. A conservative Spanish draft structure

Please distinguish between legal facts, good practice, and suggested wording.
```

## Definition of Done

- `privacidad.html` reflects the actual contact and data flow
- The text is clearer, less corporate, and less risky than a generic template
- The page still signals that a fuller legal review may happen later
