# M17B — How We Work / Safety Page

## Goal

Add a dedicated trust-and-boundaries surface explaining how Alumbra works in practice: care model, eligibility, safety framing, transfer/referral logic, and what families should expect after first contact.

## Recommended Delivery Mode

- Lead: Codex Sonnet or stronger
- Build: Haiku is acceptable only after the content model and factual inputs are fully defined
- Review: strong human review required because the wording must be accurate, calm, and non-defensive

## Dependencies

- M15 complete
- M17 complete
- M17A recommended

## PR Scope

- `content/how-we-work.json`
- A dedicated section or page according to the M17 IA decision
- Supporting HTML/CSS/JS only as needed

## Tasks

- [ ] Define the content schema for care model, boundaries, eligibility, and practical safety notes
- [ ] Explain what the team offers and what it does not offer
- [ ] Explain how independent care relates to the public or hospital system where relevant
- [ ] Explain transfer, referral, or escalation language in a calm and practical way
- [ ] Include travel-zone or availability expectations if those are part of the care model
- [ ] Keep tone warm and reassuring without sounding vague or evasive

## Definition of Done

- The site has an explicit trust-and-safety layer instead of implying it indirectly
- Families can understand the practical care model before contacting
- The wording is specific enough to reduce anxiety and broad enough to avoid brittle promises
