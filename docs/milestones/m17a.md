# M17A — FAQ Surface

## Goal

Add a focused FAQ surface that answers the highest-anxiety, highest-intent questions families are likely to have before making contact.

## Recommended Delivery Mode

- Lead: Codex Sonnet for content structure and tone
- Build: Haiku is acceptable once the question set and IA target are explicit
- Review: human approval for factual accuracy and scope discipline

## Dependencies

- M15 complete
- M17 complete

## PR Scope

- `content/faq.json`
- A homepage FAQ section or dedicated FAQ page, depending on the M17 IA decision
- Supporting HTML/CSS/JS only as needed

## Tasks

- [ ] Define the FAQ schema before implementation
- [ ] Prioritize practical questions first:
  pricing, when to contact, service fit, travel zone, languages, home birth or hospital-support boundaries, postpartum support, and first-contact expectations
- [ ] Keep answers warm, plain, and specific
- [ ] Avoid legal or medical overclaiming
- [ ] Reuse research from M13 and wording clarity from M15
- [ ] Keep the FAQ maintained and scoped; do not turn it into a dumping ground

## Definition of Done

- Families can get answers to the most common pre-contact questions without needing to message first
- The FAQ reduces anxiety and ambiguity
- The content structure is maintainable from JSON

## Open Questions

- Should the first FAQ live on the homepage, a dedicated page, or both in summary/detail form?
