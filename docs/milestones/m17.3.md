# M17.3 — `/servicios` Standalone Page + Services Content Rewrite

## Goal

Split the services surface to its own page (`/servicios.html`) and rewrite the service descriptions in the same PR. Homepage keeps 3–4 service summary cards with a "ver todos los servicios" link.

This milestone absorbs M15 (Services Content Review and Rewrite). Coupling the page split and the content rewrite means the improved copy lands directly on the new page without an intermediate migration step.

## Dependencies

- M17.2 complete

## PR Scope

- New `servicios.html` — top-level page scaffold. Loads all services from `content/services.json`.
- `content/services.json` — full rewrite of all 7 service descriptions in plain warm Spanish with structured subfields: short summary, who it is for, what it includes, pricing placeholder shown as "Precio a consultar — contacta para información actualizada"
- `js/content-loader.js` — extended for per-page services context: full list on `/servicios.html`, 3–4 featured/first cards on homepage
- Per-service anchor IDs in `servicios.html` for deep-linking: `#seguimiento-embarazo`, `#atencion-parto`, `#parto-en-casa`, `#postparto`, `#lactancia`, `#talleres`, `#tecnicas-complementarias`, `#alimentacion-complementaria`
- `@type: Service` JSON-LD on `servicios.html`
- `<title>`, meta description, og tags for `servicios.html`
- Homepage `sections/servicios.html` — becomes 3–4 summary cards with "ver todos los servicios →" link
- Internal cross-links: `servicios.html` → `/faq.html` for service-specific questions; `servicios.html` → `/#contacto`; `como-trabajamos.html` → `servicios.html` for the actual offer
