### 3 Lowest-Scoring Areas (prioritised)

1. **C19 — WhatsApp contact available** (score: 0/2 · personas: P4, P6, P7) — No `wa.me/` link exists anywhere in the HTML or contact.json; add a WhatsApp channel entry to contact.json and a tappable `wa.me/` button in the contact section and persistent nav to unlock the primary contact channel for most target personas.

2. **C5 — Price visible** (score: 0/2 · personas: P2, P11) — All six price fields are `__PLACEHOLDER__`; add at minimum one indicative price range or a "desde X euros" signal for the most common service to prevent first-time visitors from assuming unaffordability.

3. **C15 — Professional legitimacy** (score: 0/2 · personas: P9, P11, P13) — No colegiada numbers, association memberships, or insurance references appear in any team bio; add at least one formal credential per midwife to unblock trust for cautious parents and professional referrers, and to satisfy C21 for GP referrals.
